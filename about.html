The Teacher Behind the Results

A Legacy of 30 Years in Education

Shri DB Sir began his teaching journey in 1996, with a small batch of just 8 students in a single room. His unique ability to simplify the toughest concepts through everyday examples quickly earned him a reputation that no advertisement could buy — word of mouth.

Over the next 30 years, he has taught more than 10,000 students, many of whom are now doctors, engineers, teachers and business owners themselves. Hundreds have scored above 90% in board exams under his guidance, and dozens have cleared JEE, NEET and other competitive entrance examinations.

His teaching philosophy is simple: "No child is weak. Some children just need a little more time and a little more attention." This belief is the reason every batch at DB Sir Academy stays small — so that no student's doubt goes unanswered.

Even after 30 years, DB Sir still arrives at the academy before every batch, prepares fresh examples on the blackboard, and personally reviews the test paper of every single student. That is the standard that has kept families coming back for three generations.
